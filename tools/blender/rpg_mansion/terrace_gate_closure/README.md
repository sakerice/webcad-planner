# Original mansion terrace and gate closure

This candidate supplies **two new core constructions and one zero-core accessory**: a supported oak terrace, a braced oak pedestrian gate leaf, and its hollow strike receiver. It changes no accepted model, integrated catalogue, application code or remote service. Independent acceptance and coordinator visual approval are still required.

## Content and reproduction

- `authoring_sources/`: named editable native Blender construction parts
- `sources/`: canonical Blender export meshes and measured validation
- `models/`: runtime GLB, metres, +Y up, +Z front, bottom-centred origin
- `previews/`: transparent 512×512 thumb/top; `evidence/`: transparent front/rear
- `installation_proofs/`: actual unmodified GLBs in saved assemblies, measurements, four views each and focused hardware/underframe evidence
- `inputs/`: SHA-bound copies of the five accepted dependencies; all are excluded from new asset counts
- `integration-descriptors.json` and `integration-copy-map.json`: standard paths, declared finish channels and explicit copy instructions; no integration is performed

Use Blender 4.3.2. Run `blender -b -t 4 --python build.py` in this self-contained directory, then `blender -b -t 4 --python build_installation_proofs.py`, `blender -b -t 4 --python audit_source_contacts.py`, `blender -b -t 4 --python render_details.py`, `python validate_product_files.py`, and `python finalize_delivery.py`. The builder supports `-- --only supported-oak-terrace`, `-- --no-icons`, and `-- --render-only`. The proof builder supports `-- --no-render` and `-- --render-only`. Run these from the self-contained package; do not infer staging layout from the eventual repository source path.

## Terrace installation

The platform is 2400×1800 mm, with a 320.000 mm timber walking surface and 320.600 mm screw crowns. Actual accepted step geometry has the same two distinct datums. Six limestone pads contact ground, two beams bear on the pads, five joists bear on the beams, and twelve boards bear on the joists. Screws over the shortened centre joist remain over its actual support. The platform is a supported deck, not another access stair.

Place the accepted two-riser step at Blender(0,-1.2325,0)m relative to the terrace. Both product origins are at ground. The upper tread and platform timber surfaces match; the joint between timber edges is 8 mm. Step clear width is 1100 mm. The saved doorway proof reuses the actual accepted rectangular doorway bay and two actual octagon/cabochon floor tiles on an explicitly excluded foundation. Interior floor and terrace top are both 320 mm; the clear doorway is 1200×2400 mm.

The separate fixed-window proof seats the actual French bay at Blender(0,1.015,.230)m. Its 90 mm timber sill reaches 320 mm. A 1060 mm rear fascia recess and shortened centre joist clear the actual 1040 mm weather nose below the boards. The nose top is 290 mm, board underside 294 mm, with 4 mm vertical clearance. The centre joist ends at y 820 mm and the nose begins at 830 mm. The timber-to-sill joint is 12 mm. The fixed glazing and leaf frame remain closed: this proof establishes a static threshold interface, not an open passage. The window's masonry foundation is an explicitly excluded QA host.

## Gate installation

The gate is a single static, framed oak leaf with a back compression brace, two forged straps, physically connected integral hollow collars and a thumb latch. The newly authored collars are part of this delivered gate; none of the earlier excluded test collars is copied or promoted as a product.

Use accepted pier rigid transforms from the saved proof: the hinge pier at(-.3215,0,0)m and the strike pier at(1.5325,0,0)m, both rotated 180° about Blender Z. The gate and strike receiver use the opposite of their recorded `bottomCenterTranslationBlenderM` to restore the documented construction frame. Default gate elevation is 100 mm. The strike receiver's default elevation is 1095 mm; the detailed pose is necessary because it is a mounted accessory.

Accepted pin axes meet the new collar axes at x 0/y 0. The collars' bottoms bear on the actual arms at 362/1162 mm; tops are 405/1205 mm. The pins have 11 mm nominal radius and the collars 12 mm inner radius. Actual GLB radial rays in 24 directions measure clearance. The separately delivered strike receiver has two stand-off feet touching the accepted outer plate at x 1206 mm, while clearing its through-anchor tips. Its 18×18 mm open socket receives the 12 mm bolt with 3 mm nominal radial clearance.

The support plinths leave 1254 mm between their facing edges. Receiver hardware narrows the documented clear support aperture to 1183 mm. The saved leaf is closed and occupies that aperture; these measurements do not establish an animated open state or swept clearance. The accepted universal piers retain their unused outer hardware unchanged.

## Rights and limits

All new geometry is native original Blender construction, using the existing original project helper tooling. No external or paid models, images or textures are used. Materials are split into wood, metal and stone finish channels as applicable. No separate public reuse licence is granted.

These are static visual assets for manual placement. There is no native deck generation, automatic wall/floor opening, automatic snapping, traversability, gate animation, load rating or building-code certification. The accepted input models, ground, foundations and detail-view hiding of QA hosts add zero new constructions. Source verification is distinct from independent acceptance and application runtime QA.
