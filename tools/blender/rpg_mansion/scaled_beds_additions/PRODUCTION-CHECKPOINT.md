# Two additional bed construction forms

Campaign cot: four grounded X-trestle feet, brass pivot pins, long stabilizer, and taut canvas mattress support. Static deployed state; folding motion is not claimed.

Barrel-vault bed, explicitly stylized RPG furniture with no ordinary standard-bed equivalence: three laminated semicircular load ribs, curved timber roof, closed arched head, raised four-pedestal plinth and an open arched foot entrance.

Two new constructions, with 8 final views and 4 compressed editable sources. All local QA gates pass. Root accepted both repaired visuals; independent QA remains pending. Core18 and all older families remain byte-frozen. No release or publication performed.
