# Thirteen original water-room additions

This isolated expansion adds thirteen useful constructions to the three separately
accepted prototypes: sixteen new water-room assets in total. The accepted three
are never rebuilt by this directory. Existing baseline roll-top bath, pedestal
washstand and low-coupled WC are deliberately not counted again.

## Distinct construction scope

- Four baths: copper bateau on timber cradle; paneled built-in; true quadrant
  corner footprint; deep hip bath with a curved internal sitting bench.
- Four basins: mirrored single cabinet; two separate bowls on an open metal
  console; portable washing bowl and hollow water jug; two-wall corner basin.
- Three shower forms: sliding rail/handset/hose; overhead standpipe/mixer/rose;
  framed clear-glass enclosure with a real hollow tray and static closed door.
- One grounded long-reach bath filler, with a measured installed pairing using
  the accepted slipper bath. The copied QA assembly is not another bath model.
- One exposed flushometer WC with a real hollow bowl and open seat.

Capacity and footprint are explicit design differences, never palette copies.
The double console has a different support/storage construction and two actual
separate bowls; it is also labelled as a two-basin capacity group. Catalogue
demand dimensions are not claims of exact legacy replacement. No absent legacy
geometry was invented, measured, or replaced.

## Deliverables and coordinates

All geometry is original native Blender modelling, with named editable parts,
standalone authoring sources and a canonical single active export mesh. Sources
use metres, +Z up and -Y front; GLBs use metres, +Y up, +Z front and bottom-centred
origins. Bottom centring uses translation only. Authored nominal envelopes are
kept separately from floating-point vertex measurements. Separate meaningful
ceramic, metal, wood, stone, glass and hose channels appear only where used.

Every asset has 512-pixel transparent thumb/top/front/rear images. Native cavity
floor rays, intended bearing-point checks, closed-positive component volumes,
actual packed-UV overlap, strict corner-normal checks, co-oriented coplanar
surface tests, exact source-to-GLB replay, fresh image replay, and decompressed
saved-source/UI privacy gates precede independent acceptance. QA installation
floors, walls and contrasting glass markers are labelled and excluded from model
counts. The final-byte allowlist is the authoritative deliverable boundary.

## Installed bath filler

The accepted slipper bath remains the same asset. The filler is placed by its
original floor-column reference at Blender y=0.62 m, compensating explicitly for
its bottom-centre translation. The measured outlet is 85 mm inside the bath's
rear opening and 198 mm above the local rim; its feet clear the bath by 151 mm.
A downward ray from the outlet reaches the actual bowl. Pairing images and a
named two-member native QA scene provide evidence without adding a model count.

## Scope limits

These are static visual assets. Water flow, valves, doors, flushing, structural
engineering, accessibility and ergonomic certification are not provided. The
standalone baths require separate supply fixtures, and the enclosure requires
a separate shower fixture. No application files, shared catalogues, user plans,
remote repositories or deployments are modified here. Publication and packaging
remain with the parent after root visual approval and independent certification.

Run build.py with Blender for production; nominal-dimensions.json records the
fixed envelope contract. The bath_forms.py, basin_forms.py and shower_forms.py
modules are family-local authoring sources. Shared helpers are immutable copied
bytes, checked by shared-helper-hashes.json. Render scripts reopen canonical
sources; render_glass_proof.py imports the actual exported GLB shader/geometry.
