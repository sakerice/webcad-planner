Frequently asked questions

Where are my plans saved?

Inside your browser. They cannot be opened from another device or browser. To keep a plan or move it to another device, export it to a file and import it there. Clearing your browser data also deletes saved plans.

How long does a shared plan stay?

It is deleted 60 days after it was last changed or saved. If you want it deleted sooner, contact us at the address above and include the shared-editing URL.

I gave a shared-editing URL to someone I did not mean to

Anyone who knows the URL can view and change that plan. Export the plan to a file to keep your own copy, then contact us at the address above with the shared-editing URL and ask us to delete the shared plan.

The app says today's AI usage has ended

Reading a drawing and chat have a daily usage limit. It resets at 9:00 a.m. Japan time.

The plan read from my drawing differs from the drawing

The drawing is read by AI, so differences can occur. You can correct the plan on screen after it is read.

Using the service from an AI app (such as ChatGPT or Claude)

From a conversation in an AI app you can create a plan, or read and edit a shared plan. No registration or sign-in is needed.

  1. In the AI app's settings, register the following address once as a connector.
    {{mcpAddress}}
  2. To create a new plan, ask the AI app something like "Make a floor plan with house-planner". When it is done, it returns a URL that opens the plan.
  3. To edit an existing plan, open the shared-editing screen in the app, and from the section for editing from an AI app, copy the text to paste into the AI app.

Changes made by the AI app can be undone with Undo while the app screen is open. To stop changes from AI apps, turn off the setting that accepts changes from external AI on the shared-editing screen. Depending on the AI app, editing operations may not be available.

A plan created from an AI app is handled the same way as a shared plan and is deleted 60 days after it was last changed or saved. There is a daily limit on the number of plans that can be created from AI apps.
