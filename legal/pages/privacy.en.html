Privacy Policy

Effective date: {{effectiveDate}}

This policy explains how house-planner mobile ({{siteOrigin}}, "the service") handles information about its users. The service is operated by {{operatorName}} ("we"). The service is provided in Japanese; this English page is a translation of the Japanese policy, and the Japanese version prevails if the two differ.

1. No account is required

The service has no sign-in. We do not ask you to register a name, email address, phone number, or postal address, and we do not set cookies of our own.

2. What stays on your device

The following is stored inside your browser and is not sent to our server.

  • The floor plans you make: the "last plan" and the list of plans you saved by name (including a small preview image)
  • Copies of plans you opened through shared editing (the five most recent)
  • The display name you use for shared editing, and screen settings such as which panels are open

You can delete saved plans one by one from the saved list. Clearing this site's data in your browser settings deletes all of it. Because clearing browser data or changing devices cannot be undone, export any plan you want to keep to a file.

3. What is sent to our server

Information is sent to our server (which runs on services provided by Cloudflare, Inc.) only when you use one of the features below, and only what that feature needs.

Reading a floor plan drawing

What is sent
The floor plan image or PDF you choose, the note you add, and images of the resulting plan and exterior that the screen draws so the reading can be checked
Purpose
To build a plan from the drawing. Our server passes these to the AI providers listed in section 4
Retention
Not stored on our server

Making or editing a plan by chat

What is sent
The text you type and the contents of the plan at that time
Purpose
To edit the plan as requested. Our server passes these to the AI provider listed in section 4
Retention
Not stored on our server

Shared editing

What is sent
The plan you share, a display name (it does not need to be your real name), and a random value created by your browser
Purpose
To share changes in real time among people who open the same URL
Retention
The plan is deleted 60 days after it was last changed or saved. The display name and random value are used only while you are connected

Editing a plan from an AI app

What is sent
The operations, and any new plan, sent on your behalf by an AI app (such as ChatGPT or Claude) in which you registered this service as a connector
Purpose
To read, create, and edit a shared plan from a conversation in the AI app. The contents of the plan (such as room names) are returned to that AI app
Retention
Same as shared editing (60 days)

Common to all features

What is sent
IP address, browser information, the URL you open, and the approximate region derived from the IP address
Purpose
To return pages, prevent abusive bulk use, and investigate failures
Retention
The AI features and plan creation from AI apps have a daily usage limit. To count it, we keep the IP address (for IPv6, its leading part) for the current and previous day only. Access records used to investigate failures are deleted within 7 days at most

Anyone who knows a shared-editing URL can view and change that plan. Give the URL only to people you want to share it with. If a drawing shows a name or address, hide it before you use the reading feature or shared editing.

4. Companies that process information for us

We do not provide information to third parties without consent, except where the law permits. To run the service we entrust processing to the following companies.

  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States): operates the server and stores shared plans. An AI model running on Cloudflare is used to tidy up a plan after reading (room names and fixtures)
  • Anthropic, PBC (United States): receives the content described in section 3 for reading drawings and for chat
  • Google LLC (United States): when you choose a PDF, reduced-size page images are passed to Google Cloud's AI (processed in a Japan region) to find the page and area that contains the drawing
  • OpenAI OpCo, LLC (United States): receives the same content instead, only when the AI of the two companies above is temporarily unavailable, for example because it is overloaded or down

Under their terms, these AI providers do not use the content they receive to train their AI models. A provider may retain the content it receives for a limited period to monitor for abuse.
